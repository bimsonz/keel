### Changed

- The console's project tabs are a code host's: Code, Changes, Pipeline, Mission Control, Insights and Settings. A project's decisions, conflicts and questions are Insights' pages, and a conflict's or a question's own page belongs to Changes.
- A change's page reads as a pull request: an Open, Landed or Closed pill, Conversation, Checks and Files changed, and a status box first with at most one action. A question it waits on is ruled there, in words, by one button per answer that says what it does, after a confirmation.
- The Changes list reads as a pull request list: Open and Closed, then Waiting on a ruling, Bounced and Landed.
- The header's top right is an account menu for someone signed in and a Sign in button for a visitor.
- A project's home leads with what waits on a person and has an About column. A file's page names the decisions Keel's `why` gives for it.
- A change parked because its agent's lease lapsed reads "Waiting on its agent", never as waiting on a person.
