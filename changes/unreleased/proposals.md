### Added

- **Proposals from forks.** A commit of a fork is proposed back to the project it was forked from: `POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/proposals` (`propose`), listed by `GET /v1/projects/{projectId}/proposals` (`proposals`), moved to another commit by its proposer (`POST /v1/changes/{changeId}/proposal/commits`, `updateProposal`), approved at the commit read (`…/proposal/approval`, `approveProposal`), asked for changes (`…/proposal/changes-requested`, `requestProposalChanges`) and closed (`…/proposal/close`, `closeProposal`). Each is an MCP tool. A proposal is a change of the project that no agent holds: `Change.agentId` is absent on one, which has `origin: proposal` and `proposal {fork, commit, proposer, approved?}`. It is checked under the project's own checks at its trunk head and waits, unverified, for a maintainer's approval of the commit it carries; a new commit clears the approval. Its events: `ChangeProposed`, `ProposalUpdated`, `ProposalApproved`, `ProposalChangesRequested`, and `ChangeParked {reason: awaiting-approval}`.
- **Who may.** On a public project any signed-in person proposes from a fork they work in, and so does an agent working in the fork that a person sponsors (`sponsor-required` otherwise). A person, or a sponsored agent, holding `maintain` approves; the proposer alone moves it; the proposer or an approver closes it. At most five open per proposer per project (`proposal.limit`). A private project takes none, and `proposals: open|closed` in a project's settings (open by default) closes a public one.
- **Sync a fork.** `POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/sync` (`Operator_syncUpstream`) syncs a fork with the project it was forked from, as it syncs an import: to its upstream's trunk head when the fork holds nothing of its own, otherwise to a merge commit Keel makes and signs. A path both changed apart is refused 409 `trunk.sync_conflicts`, naming it, with nothing moved. Its owners sync it only while they may read its upstream. `TrunkSyncStarted` and `TrunkSynced` name a fork's upstream as `{project}` and the merged head as `merged`.
- **In the console:** a review box on a proposal waiting for approval, a Proposals filter on Changes, and on a fork's code a bar with **Sync fork** and **Propose**.
