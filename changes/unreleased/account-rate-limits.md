### Added

- **Per-account daily limits on proposals, their moves, issues and issue comments.** Each counts against the account that makes it: a person's own namespace, or for an agent the person who sponsors it, so a sponsor's agents share one allowance. By default an account opens 10 proposals, moves its proposals to another commit 50 times, and opens 10 issues and 200 issue comments a UTC day; whoever runs the instance sets other limits in `KEEL_QUOTAS` (`defaults`, or per namespace), under the new quota kinds `proposals`, `proposal-moves`, `issues` and `issue-comments`. Past the limit, `propose`, `updateProposal`, `openIssue` and `commentOnIssue` answer 429 `rate.limited` with Retry-After until the next UTC midnight. A retry under the same Idempotency-Key counts once, and one sent after the count starts again is decided again. A proposal or move refused for its commit (its pack too large, or past its fork's repo bytes) counts nothing, and a proposal refused for the day frees its place among the five a proposer may hold open.
