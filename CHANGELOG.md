# Changelog

Keel's contracts: the public API (`packages/spec`, emitted to `packages/contracts/openapi.json`), the MFP schema it imports, the ports in `packages/core/src/application/ports`, the DO RPC contracts in `apps/*/src/rpc`, and the runner protocol in `packages/runner-protocol`.

From `contracts-v0` on, every contract change gets a line here. Contract changes are additive (a new operation, an optional field, a new port or RPC method); a breaking change is listed under **Changed (breaking)** and says what a caller must do.

## Unreleased

### Added

- `ChangeRpc.awaitPlan({subscriber: "workflow:<Name>/<instanceId>"})` (for KeelWorldCheck; additive RPC). It answers `{change, projectId}` exactly when core's `planStateOf` reads anything but Pending from the change: a plan compiled for its current submission, or a state no plan is coming in. While its Precheck is still to answer (submitted or deriving, whatever plan hash an earlier submission left) it answers `{waiting: true}` and subscribes the instance once, in the read's transaction, to the change's next PlanCompiled, ChangeDrifted, ChangeBounced, ChangeWithdrawn or ChangeSuperseded (`AWAIT_PLAN_HEARS`). An unknown change is ChangeNotFound. Core's `awaitPlan` and `awaitSubmission` are one read-and-subscribe under two rules; the waiting answer's type is `Awaiting` (it was `AwaitingSubmission`).
- A project's deploy targets. A deploy target is a record `{name, provider, accountId, credential}`: `name` is what a repo's `.keel/deploy` gives (a handle's shape, unique in its project), `provider` is `cloudflare`, `accountId` is 32 hex characters, and `credential` is a `CredentialId` (`crd_<ULID>`, a new id prefix): a reference into the org's credential store, minted when a secret is stored, so the record cannot hold a secret.
  - Two events on the project, for members only: `DeployTargetSet {projectId, target, by: UserId}` and `DeployTargetCleared {projectId, name, by: UserId}`. The catalogue goes from 104 to 106 events; `events.tsp` has the rows and the models `DeployTarget`, `DeployProvider`, `DeployTargetName`, `CloudflareAccountId` and `CredentialId`.
  - Pure decisions in `domain/delivery/deploy-target.ts`: `setDeployTarget` (refused `credential-not-in-org` when the credential is not in the project's own org, `too-many-targets` past `MAX_DEPLOY_TARGETS` = 16; setting a target to what it is commits nothing), `clearDeployTarget` (refused `not-set`), `evolveDeployTargets` and `resolveDeployTarget`, which gives `Deployer.plan` the record a name stands for. The refusal is `DeployTargetRefused {name, reason}`.
  - `authorize` decides `ManageCredentials` (an owner of the org), beside `ManageDeployTargets` (an owner of the project's org), which was already there.
- The Trunk counts what it lands against its project's org (PushRefused's wiring, part 2). Before every push of a group, `pushPromotion` weighs the group's pack (`TrunkWriter.packBytes`) and admits it into the org's `repo-bytes` with `OrgUsage.admit(org, "repo-bytes", bytes − 32, "promotion:<sha>")`, so the retries of one group count once. A pack over what is left commits the domain's `PushRefused {reason: repo-bytes-quota}` with trunk still on the WAL's old head: each batch gets its `PromotionDeclined`, nothing is pushed, and the queue moves on. A group that ends declined after it was admitted gives its bytes back first (`record(…, −bytes, "giveback:<sha>")`): on a refused push, and when a frozen trunk is resumed onto any head but the group's own commit. `initializeTrunk` counts the repo's whole history at its first head, once (`record(…, "create:<projectId>")`). The org is `ProjectOrgs.byId`'s; a project with no place yet is neither weighed nor counted (until the tenancy cutover gives every project one), so a project is placed before its trunk is initialized. The Trunk DO's services gain `OrgUsage` (over `ORG_USAGE`) and `ProjectOrgs` (D1's `projects` table, migration 0008).
- `TrunkWriter.historyBytes(at) → number` (additive port method): the length of the pack of everything `at` reaches in trunk, counted as it streams and never held. GitCore gains `uploadPackBytes(remote, wants, haves)`, the streamed twin of `uploadPack`, equal to its pack's length wherever the answer's chunks end. `makeTrunkWriterMemory()` gains `holds(at, bytes)` and `weighed()`. `PACK_OVERHEAD_BYTES` (32) is exported from `@keeltown/core/application/integration`.
- The OrgUsage object (new, not yet called): Durable Object `OrgUsageDO` on binding `ORG_USAGE` (wrangler migration `v2`, one new SQLite class), addressed `usage:<orgId>`, so one object serialises one org's admissions. RPC `OrgUsageRpc {admit({kind, amount, key}), record({kind, amount, key}), settings(), configure({overrides, halted})}`; the org is the object's name, never a parameter. Adapter `OrgUsageRpc(env.ORG_USAGE)`, which runs the whole OrgUsage contract through the real object. Keys are forgotten a week after their first answer by the object's `usage prune` deadline, armed a day after a count and daily while any key is held.
- An org's own quota settings, on the `OrgUsage` port (additive port methods): `settings(org) → UsageSettings {overrides: Partial<Quotas>, halted}` and `configure(org, settings)`, which replaces them whole. `admit` now decides under them: the org's own limit comes before the instance's default, and a halted org's spend kinds are refused with `reason: halted`. A limit that is negative or not finite is a defect. The settings live beside the counts (in the OrgUsage object), so the `SetQuotas` route needs only `authorize` and `configure`. Contract cases `[orgusage.contract.settings|override|halted]`. `pruneUsageKeys` answers `{forgotten, kept}` (it answered a number); `usageAddress(org)` and `usageOrg(address)` name the object.
- The `SyntaxSource` port and its adapter (composed in Precheck's runtime, called by nothing yet). `SyntaxSource.facts(repo, commit, seeds) → SyntaxReading {facts: SourceFacts, skipped: {path, reason}[], parser}`, failing `RepoNotFound`, `ObjectNotFound` or `SyntaxUnavailable {reason: too-many-seeds | too-many-bytes | too-many-failures | parser-starting | parser-failed}`.
  - The path rule is the adapter's: it parses the seeds and follows their relative imports to a fixpoint, breadth-first, up to 400 files, 512 KB a file and 8 MB a call. A caller gives the files a change touches and the files the live anchors name.

- `TrunkWriter.packBytes(from, to) → number | PackTooLarge` (additive port method, unwired): the length in bytes of the pack `push(from, to)` relays, asked of staging with the same want and the same have and counted as the pack itself, with none of its transport framing. Trunk's repo grows by that less the 32 bytes of a pack's header and trailer. A pack over the receive-pack cap is `PackTooLarge`, compared with the cap exactly; it reads staging and writes nothing. GitCore gains `relayPack(source, move, options)`, the pack a `relay` of the same move sends (`relay` now calls it). `makeTrunkWriterMemory().weigh(to, bytes)` sets what `packBytes` answers, and a candidate nobody weighed weighs an empty pack's 32 bytes.

Once drafted, it starts one KeelWorldCheck per world, which records each party's suite in its world as WorldCheckRan: evidence for people, never admission. `workflow:Probe/<instanceId>` sends a party's next move to a waiting instance as `change-<changeId>`. - What Precheck derives reaches the subjects (for Precheck's derive step; additive RPC and port methods).
  - `ChangeRpc.effectsDerived({revision, instanceId, effects: DerivedEffect[], derivationRef})` and port `ChangePrecheck.derived(changeId, {revision, instanceId, effects, derivationRef})` run the domain's `DeriveEffects`: EffectsDerived alone, once per Precheck pass while the change derives. It is idempotent by `<instanceId>:effectsDerived`. A second derivation in the pass, a stale revision or any other state is ChangeStateRefused, and an unknown change is ChangeNotFound. The event's `affected` is each derived effect's subject, and in the same transaction the change subscribes those subjects and their ancestors, scoped to itself, so a subject the intent never named hears that very event.
  - The Subject DO hears EffectsDerived (`SUBJECT_HEARS`). What it holds of a change gains the change's derived effects at or under its key. A derivation later on the change's log replaces an earlier one (two passes can share a revision), and an earlier one arriving late is ignored. Fallout judges declared and derived effects alike, in both directions. A claim both declared and derived is one effect, judged as the agent's own. A conflict's `origin` is `opinion` when the effect it was judged on is a model's opinion alone, else `fallout`. A subject first held through a derived effect is minted from it (`mintedBy: clef` for clef's, else `agent`).
  - A fallout pair is now identified by its claims (the domain's `claimKey`, exported for this) and the premise's strength, not by the whole propositions: anchors and gloss no longer make a second pair. Conflict ids therefore differ from those the first fallout push derived.
  - `PrecheckWorkspace.intent`, and `intent` on `ChangeRpc.workspace()`'s answer: the change's current intent. It is required on the port, so a test that builds a `PrecheckWorkspace` gives one.
  - `Projects.liveAnchors(projectId): SubjectAnchors[]` over `ProjectRpc.liveAnchors()`: where the project's live changes anchor their subjects in code (premises, declared effects and derived effects that carry anchors), each subject once with each anchor once, in one order. A derive step calls it inside the step, since a Workflow step's result is capped at 1 MiB. The Project DO's live index now places a change's derivation by its position on the change's log, as the Subject DO does.

The Change machine gains the command `DeriveEffects {effects: DerivedEffect[], derivationRef}`: taken only while the change is Deriving and only when its current pass has recorded none, it commits `EffectsDerived {changeId, revision, effects, derivationRef}` alone, each proposition in canonical form; anything else is ChangeStateRefused with a read of the change, and nothing is committed. `CompilePlan` loses `derivation?` and now commits `DriftDetected?` and `PlanCompiled` only, so `EffectsDerived` precedes both in an earlier commit and is also on the log when drift sends the change to Drifted. `Change` gains `derivationRef?: string`, set from `EffectsDerived` and cleared by the next `PrecheckStarted`: once per Precheck pass, not per revision, because a bounced change resubmitted without a revision is a new commit whose effects must be derived again. `evolveChange` takes `EffectsDerived` only at Deriving, at the change's revision, with none recorded in the pass. - `SubjectRpc.rely` and its codec: a change relies on a subject through its own Change DO only (`ChangeRpc.rely`), which subscribes the subject.
- `LaneRpc.enqueue` and `LaneCodecs.enqueue`, which nothing called: a change joins its lane by its ChangeQueued only (no callers).

## contracts-v0 (10 Oct 2026)

Changes since `contracts-v0-rc1` (`3e7c7f1`, 10 Oct 2026). Everything is additive or a fix except the `EventOutbox` reshape, which landed before the freeze.

### Added

- `AgentDirectory.verifyCredential(clientId, secret)`: the adapter hashes and compares in constant time, so a client secret never leaves it.
- `declaredEffects?: MFP.Proposition[]` on declaring and revising a change. Advisory only: Keel derives effects from the diff.
- Kernel: pure content hashing (`ContentHash`, `sha256:` + 64 hex) and glob matching.
- Identity: every agent is enrolled in exactly one project. `Agent.projectId`, `Principal.projectId`, the token's `prj` claim, `AgentEnrolled.projectId`, the D1 column `agents.project_id` (migration 0002) and `projectId` on the API's `AgentProfile`.
- Ports `Changes` (the isolate's gateway to the Change DOs: `declare`, `submit`, `get`, `awaitSubmission`) and `Inbox` (`read`, long-polling), each with an in-memory adapter.
- Change RPC `submission(waitMs)`, answering `SubmissionStatus {change, commitSha}`; AgentSession RPC `reserveChange({idempotencyKey, fingerprint})` (a declare retried with the same key reaches the same change; the same key with another body is `IdempotencyConflict`) and `inbox({cursor, waitMs})`.
- Identity domain: `IdempotencyConflict`, and the `AgentNotice` and `InboxPage` schemas (the `Notifier` port re-exports `AgentNotice`, unchanged in shape).
- `CodeHost.diffTrees(repo, base, head)`: the leaves that differ between two commits, as `git diff-tree -r --no-renames` reports them, as `TreeChange` rows in bytewise path order. Unchanged subtrees are skipped by id.
- `CodeHost.delete(repo)`: idempotent. Workspaces are collected by deleting their repo.
- `CodeHost.info(repo)`: `{remote, defaultBranch}`. The remote's host carries the account id, so it goes only to a grant's holder and is never logged.
- `WorkspaceGrant.baseSha` and `WorkspaceGranted.baseSha`: the workspace's head at grant time, which is the change's base. Precheck diffs against it, so a mirror that lags trunk can't leave the base missing.
- WorkspacePool: `grant` is idempotent per holder; a retry returns the same workspace with a freshly minted token. `release(workspaceId, reason?)` takes the `WorkspaceCollected` reason (`landed`, `withdrawn` or `expired`; `withdrawn` when absent). The RPC gains `configure({source, target})` and `status()`.
- `commit()` in the ports: read the clock, then in one `EntityStore` transaction either return what the cause already committed or run the decision and append its events (gap-free `seq` per entity, dedupe keys `<kind>:<id>/<cause>/<type>#<n>`, `notified[]` from the subscription table at commit time). A refusal rolls back every write.
- `EventReceiverRpc.receive(envelopes)`: every Keel Durable Object receives events through this one method; `ChangeRpc`, `AgentSessionRpc` and the other DO contracts extend it. `DeliveryReceipt` and `EventCodecs` in `apps/keel/src/rpc/events.ts`.
- `CodeHostFixture` in `@keeltown/testing`: `commit(repo, ref, files)` and `scratch()`, provided by `makeCodeHostMemory().layer`. Tests seed repos through it, because `CodeHost` never writes git content.

### Fixed

- Scopes are per operation. Each operation now demands only its own scope; the OAuth flow model used to list all four, which overrode the operations and made every operation demand all of them.
- The Change RPC `submit` codec now carries submit's own errors, `ChangeNotFound | LeaseStale | ChangeStateRefused | CommitNotInWorkspace`. It had `RevisionMismatch`, which belongs to revise alone.

### Changed

- `EventOutbox` is reshaped around the outbox fan-out: `append(spec, at, drafts)`, `committed(entity, cause)`, `log(entity)`, `subscribe`, `unsubscribe`, `subscribers(type)` and `receive(dedupeKey, at)`, with its in-memory adapter on the in-memory `EntityStore` so one transaction covers both. It replaces rc1's `append`/`pending`/`acknowledge`.
- The MFP library is pinned at `github:bimsonz/mfp#v0.1.0-pre.3`.
- `CodeHost` reads fail with typed errors instead of dying: `RepoNotFound` (every read, `diffTrees`, `mintToken`, and `fork`'s source), `RefNotFound` (`head`), and `ObjectNotFound` (`readTree`, `readBlob`, and `diffTrees` when the repo lacks `base` or `head`). This narrows the error channels, but the port had no callers yet. Transport failures stay defects. `TreeEntry.type` gains `"commit"` for submodules, and `readTree` is documented as the commit's recursive listing.
- The in-memory `CodeHost` stores real git objects through gitcore. It fails `RepoNotFound` for a repo it never created or has deleted; it used to create the repo on the first read. Its `seed()` is replaced by `CodeHostFixture`.
